# Why This Demo — the customer context

This scenario was chosen because it maps onto what **Thomas Jefferson University Health System
(Jefferson Health)** is publicly living through as of 2025–2026. The demo data is **synthetic and
fictionalized** (fictional "Metro / Valley / Cherry" networks), but the *shape* of the problem —
labor cost pressure + post-merger data fragmentation — is deliberately true-to-life.

## What the public record shows (2025–2026)

- **Losing money, and it's accelerating.** ~**$196M operating loss in FY2025**, worsening to a
  **$252.6M operating loss (-1.9% margin) through Q3 FY2026** (vs. a $29.3M loss the prior-year
  period). *(Becker's Hospital Review)*
- **Cutting labor.** ~**600–700 layoffs (~1% of a 65,000 workforce)** announced Oct 2025, effective
  **Jan 2026**, framed as "targeted workforce reductions" amid "significant financial headwinds" —
  including **108 cuts at Jefferson Cherry Hill Hospital** (NJ WARN notice). *(Fierce Healthcare,
  Healthcare Finance News)* — this is why the demo's worst campus is nicknamed "Cherry."
- **Newly, hugely merged.** Combined with **Lehigh Valley Health Network (LVHN)** into a
  **~32-hospital, ~$15B system** spanning Eastern PA into South Jersey, plus a **Tower Health**
  affiliation (2026). Becker's calls it a **"crossroads moment"**: three years getting *bigger*,
  now the work is getting *better* — i.e., integrating formerly-separate systems. *(Becker's)*
- **Already investing in governed AI.** Jan 2026 partnership with **Qualified Health** to deploy a
  *governed* generative-AI platform enterprise-wide. *(Becker's)*

## How that shaped the demo

- **Labor cost + cross-system fragmentation** is the board-level pain → the demo targets
  **travel-agency nurse spend** driven by a **scheduling** failure, using **Epic + PeopleSoft +
  Kronos + a manager's Excel**.
- **Post-merger integration** → the deliberate **cost-center-format drift** and **float-pool
  mis-booking** data-quality traps (three networks, three ID conventions).
- **Framing tips for the room:**
  1. **Position against layoffs, not with them.** Frame it as finding *avoidable* agency/OT spend
     before cutting more headcount — CFO-friendly and morale-safe.
  2. **Complement Qualified Health, don't compete.** They already bought a *governed* AI platform,
     so lead with the "analyst-in-the-loop, verify-before-you-trust" ethos of Vibe Analytics rather
     than "the AI gives you the answer."
